01 · Source

Where are you located?

Village/district, state and the practical pickup or dispatch point.

02 · Product

What do you handle?

Tender, green round, mature, mixed varieties or another coconut format.

03 · Capacity

How much and how often?

Approximate daily/weekly/monthly capacity and harvest or procurement frequency.

04 · Handling

How is it prepared?

Whole, dehusked, sharp-cut, sorted, packed or another preparation method.

05 · Logistics

How can it move?

Pickup availability, transport access, packaging and typical dispatch capability.

06 · Proof

What can you share?

Current photos/video, basic business details and any relevant documentation.
