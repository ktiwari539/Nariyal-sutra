What we mean by fresh coconut water

The drink stays inside the coconut.

For our current fresh-coconut offering, coconut water is enjoyed from the tender fruit rather than sold as a shelf-stable bottle, powder or concentrate. That distinction matters when comparing products, serving formats and commercial requirements. Depending on region and buying context, the same fresh-fruit category may also be described as tender coconut water, green coconut water or young coconut water.

Common questions

Fresh coconut water, without vague claims.

Does Nariyal Sutra sell bottled coconut water?

Our current storefront focuses on fresh tender and green coconuts, including whole and sharp-cut options. Bottled, concentrated or powdered coconut water should not be assumed unless we explicitly confirm it for a specific commercial enquiry.

Can I order tender coconuts for an event, hotel, café or office?

Yes. Use the smart enquiry desk for quantity, destination, required date and serving preference. Stock, delivery and final presentation are confirmed for the requirement rather than promised generically.

How much coconut water is inside one tender coconut?

There is no exact amount that applies to every fruit. Water volume naturally varies by cultivar, size and harvest stage. Product pages may show an approximate reference, but the individual coconut remains a natural product.

Can I request sharp-cut, drink-ready coconuts?

Yes, as a serving preference. Availability depends on current product stock, handling and the specific order or commercial requirement.

Do you own the farms shown in your sourcing stories?

Nariyal Sutra is a buyer and supplier working with farmer and supplier partners. Our sourcing pages explain the supply approach and should not be read as a claim that Nariyal Sutra owns every farm, store or packing location associated with a requirement.
