Before You Order

Quick Answers

The essentials customers usually need before confirming an order.

What is the minimum bulk order?

Bulk pricing starts at 10 pieces. The live bulk rate is ₹45 per coconut.

How is delivery timing confirmed?

Delivery depends on your location, stock and order size. Send the order request and we confirm serviceability and timing on WhatsApp before fulfilment.

How can I pay?

Cash on Delivery may be available for supported orders. Verified UPI details or a secure card/netbanking payment link are shared only after the order is confirmed.

Do you handle wholesale or export enquiries?

Yes. Large-volume and export enquiries receive a custom quote based on quantity, packaging, destination and logistics requirements.

Why can coconut size and water volume vary?

Coconuts are natural produce, so size, water volume, sweetness and appearance vary by variety and harvest stage. Approximate product descriptions are not fixed lab specifications.
