Fulfilment policy

Delivery, Cancellation & Replacement

Delivery serviceability

Delivery is considered based on the requested destination, product availability, order quantity and available logistics options. Exact serviceability and timing are confirmed after the order/enquiry is reviewed.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges are confirmed after checking the delivery location, logistics/vendor availability, order quantity and the total shipment weight/packaging requirement. The website does not promise a fixed delivery charge before this review.

Cancellation

An order may be cancelled before it is dispatched. Once dispatch has taken place, cancellation may no longer be possible because fulfilment and logistics costs may already have been committed.

Damaged or spoiled coconuts

If coconuts arrive damaged, spoiled or otherwise unsuitable, contact us promptly with the order reference and supporting photographs/details. Replacement or refund requests are reviewed case-by-case based on the condition reported, available evidence and the circumstances of the order.
