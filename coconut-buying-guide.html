What are tender and green coconuts?

A tender coconut is a young coconut containing natural coconut water, with a soft or developing interior depending on its maturity. Green coconuts are often sold for drinking coconut water, but the appearance and amount of water naturally vary with variety and maturity.

Unlike packaged coconut-water drinks, water served straight from an opened tender coconut comes from the fruit itself. Nariyal Sutra describes its offering as fresh coconut water and tender or green coconuts; see the coconut water guide for the product story.

Frequently asked buying questions

Check the current product selection and confirm the order requirements. Quantity, fruit format, destination and delivery arrangements can affect the final commercial quote. This page does not publish a fixed price.

How do I request bulk coconut supply in India?

Start with the bulk supply page. Include the destination, expected number of coconuts, schedule and intended use so the team can assess feasibility.

Can a hotel, café or event planner order fresh coconuts?

Yes, they can make an events and hospitality enquiry. Acceptance depends on quantities, date, preparation needs and service area.

Does Nariyal Sutra deliver to every country?

No worldwide delivery guarantee is made. Nariyal Sutra is India-based and accepts wholesale and international trade enquiries; destination, volume, documentation and logistics require individual confirmation.

Where do the coconuts come from?

Nariyal Sutra describes sourcing through grower and supplier partners. Read about its sourcing process or see the supplier partnership information. Origin and availability should be confirmed for a specific consignment.

Is coconut water a medical treatment?

No medical or disease-treatment claims are made here. Coconut water is a beverage; people with dietary or medical restrictions should consult an appropriate professional about their needs.
