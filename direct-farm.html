A note from Nariyal Sutra

Keep the source story real and accountable.

We prefer clear source details, current product visuals, quantity, destination and handling needs over generic stock claims. The aim is simple: keep the sourcing conversation practical, transparent and connected to the requirement, the current product and the people in the sourcing conversation.

Before we commit

Six details make a sourcing brief useful.

We do not need a perfect presentation. We need enough current information to match the coconut, quantity and delivery reality to the requirement.

01 · SourceRegion & source context

Growing region or supplier location, plus who is handling the current lot.

02 · ProductVariety & condition

Tender, green round, whole or prepared preference and the condition available now.

03 · VisualsCurrent product photos

Recent visuals help avoid generic stock assumptions and make selection faster.

04 · VolumeQuantity & frequency

One-time quantity, recurring requirement or expected supply capacity.

05 · DestinationCity & delivery window

Destination, timing and access details affect what can be committed responsibly.

06 · HandlingServe & logistics needs

Whole, sharp-cut, event presentation, packing and transport requirements.
