Customer information

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, process order requests, coordinate delivery, communicate order status and operate the owner dashboard.

Information we may collect

  • Name, WhatsApp/phone number and optional email address.
  • Delivery address, city, state, PIN, country and optional GPS coordinates when you choose to share location.
  • Product, quantity, payment preference, enquiry details and delivery requirements.
  • Basic website analytics that do not intentionally include your personal contact details as analytics event parameters.

How we use it

Information is used to respond to your request, confirm stock and serviceability, prepare quotes, coordinate fulfilment, provide order tracking/status updates and maintain business records.

Location

GPS access is optional. If you decline browser location permission, you can enter the delivery address manually. Location data is used only to help identify the requested delivery destination.

Service providers

We use service providers such as Firebase for website data storage, EmailJS for transactional email and WhatsApp for customer communication. To make address entry easier, country/state/city lists may be loaded from a third-party location-data CDN, and a PIN/postal code may be sent to a postal lookup service when you use the smart location feature. These services receive only the information needed for that function. You can always choose the location manually instead.

Retention and requests

We retain records as reasonably required for order/enquiry handling and applicable business or legal obligations. To request correction or deletion of information, contact nariyalsutra@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

Important: Do not send payment PINs, OTPs, passwords or card credentials through the enquiry form, email or WhatsApp.
